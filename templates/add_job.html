"Save to tracker" button for your browser

For sites that block the link above (StepStone, Indeed, Glassdoor...): it sends the job page you're looking at straight from your browser, so there's nothing for the site to block.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it: Save to tracker
  3. On any job page, click it. A tab opens here showing what was saved. This app has to be running.
